What it does

  • Every state, not just the happy path

    Loading, empty, declined, pending and failed all have their own screens.

  • Pay at any terminal

    The terminal sends the amount in local currency. You pick which coin pays, and the fee is already in the total.

  • One card, one balance

    The Pay with panel sits on top of the card, so you always see which coin a tap will use.

  • Guards on every transfer

    Wrong-network addresses are caught before you continue. Review shows the full address, and Max leaves room for the fee.
